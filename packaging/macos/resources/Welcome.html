BatteryBridge Developer Preview

This installer adds BatteryBridge to Applications and installs its background charging service. macOS will request an administrator password.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac and macOS 11 or newer. Charging control is experimental and depends on your firmware. Installation does not guarantee that your Mac accepts a charge limit.

During an update, BatteryBridge closes and the service restarts. Your saved charging settings are kept. On a first installation the charge limit is off.

Only local administrator accounts can change charging settings. The service continues running after you close the app and starts again after a reboot.
